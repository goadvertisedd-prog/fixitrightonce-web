Client protections

OUR COMMITMENTS

Proposed commercial terms, to be set out in the final written New Jersey agreement and subject to attorney review. They are not legally binding until that agreement is signed.

01

TRANSPARENT BUDGET

The selected package, scope, materials allowance and price are documented before work begins.

02

NO SURPRISE MARKUPS

Materials are purchased within the approved materials budget. Receipts are maintained.

03

WRITTEN CHANGE ORDERS

Work outside the agreed scope, or hidden conditions requiring additional work, needs your written approval before proceeding — except where immediate action is legally or reasonably necessary to prevent damage or a safety risk.

04

UNUSED CONTINGENCY

Unused contingency funds belong to the client and are credited or returned according to the final accounting.

05

PROJECT UPDATES

Reasonable progress updates and documentation are provided during the project.

06

LICENSED TRADE WORK

Licensed trade work such as HVAC, electrical and plumbing is performed by appropriately licensed NJ trade contractors, subject to final verification and contract requirements.

07

INSURANCE

Subcontractors are required to maintain their own applicable general liability insurance, with certificates provided before starting where required by the final agreement.

08

FINAL WALKTHROUGH

You receive a final walkthrough and punch-list opportunity before final completion.

09

PERMITS / CLOSEOUT

Applicable permits and inspections are addressed and closed before final project completion where required.

10

WARRANTY

Proposed workmanship warranty: 30 days from final sign-off for parts and labor, subject to exclusions and the final written agreement.

Manufacturer warranties remain subject to manufacturer terms and registration requirements.

11

DELAY CREDIT

Proposed: a $450 one-time credit if FixItRightOnce or its subcontractors cause an unexcused delay beyond the agreed completion date.

Valid extensions: severe weather affecting exterior work; municipal permit/inspection scheduling; client-requested changes; material backorders; hidden conditions; other documented circumstances outside FixItRightOnce’s reasonable control.

Payment & escrow

HOW YOUR MONEY IS PROTECTED

A proposed structure in which funds move only against an approved budget and verified progress.

  1. Client selects a package.
  2. Final scope, materials allowance, price and target completion date are placed in the written agreement.
  3. Project funds are held through a neutral escrow arrangement where agreed by the parties.
  4. Materials funds are released according to the approved budget.
  5. Receipts are maintained.
  6. Labor is paid according to verified project stages.
  7. Final payment occurs after the final walkthrough, punch list, applicable permit closeout and required resale inspection/closeout.
  8. Unused approved funds and contingency are reconciled and returned or credited according to the final agreement.

Due diligence

BEFORE WE START

Items to verify before signing and before work begins. These are project due-diligence items, not legal conclusions.

  • New A/C and furnace sizing is confirmed by the licensed NJ HVAC contractor before equipment is ordered.
  • Siding and old ceiling tiles may contain asbestos. This is accounted for in the plan and price: both are prepared and painted as part of the painting scope, with appropriate precautions.
  • Lead-safe / EPA RRP requirements apply because the home was built in 1958.
  • The sewer line has not yet been camera-scoped.
  • The shower valve/cartridge is currently failed and leaking.
  • NJ Home Improvement Contractor registration and the contracting structure must be confirmed before signing.
  • NJ-licensed trade contractors must handle applicable HVAC, electrical and plumbing work.
  • The final NJ contract should receive appropriate legal review.
  • Mount Ephraim resale / certificate-of-occupancy requirements must be confirmed before closing.
  • Permit and inspection requirements must be confirmed with the Borough or other appropriate authority.

The arrangement

WHY THIS WORKS FOR BOTH SIDES

We want this project to work financially and operationally for both sides. Our goal is not to maximize the invoice; it is to complete the agreed scope efficiently, protect the owner’s budget, and deliver a property that is ready for the next stage.

For the investor

  • Controlled scope and a defined price
  • Transparent accounting and documented materials
  • Contingency protection — unused funds are yours
  • Written change orders
  • Milestone payments tied to verified progress
  • Final walkthrough and proposed warranty
  • Professional project management

For FixItRightOnce

  • A clear scope and an approved budget
  • Defined payment milestones
  • The ability to coordinate licensed subcontractors
  • Reasonable protection against unknown hidden conditions and client-requested changes
  • No obligation to absorb genuinely hidden conditions that could not be identified before opening walls, floors or other concealed areas
