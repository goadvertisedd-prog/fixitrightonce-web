Coverage

What’s covered, and what isn’t.

Included at no charge

  • Check of all accessible doors and windows (operation, locks, glass, screens, seals), steps, railings, porch, visible drainage and the half bath; needed repairs reported with photos and priced before work
  • Fresh caulk and grout touch-ups in kitchen and baths, finished flooring edges and transitions, selections approved by the owner before ordering, thorough final cleaning, final walkthrough and punch list
  • All items in this scope delivered working; existing fixtures not replaced are tested and any failures reported and priced

What’s not covered

  • Home inspection: the base covers everything identified in our walkthrough (the scope on this page). Conditions we could not inspect, such as the sewer line and anything hidden behind walls, floors or ceilings, are priced separately if found.
  • Lower-level bedroom window: if the borough or an inspector requires a larger emergency-escape (egress) window for the lower-level bedroom, that work is priced separately.
  • Asbestos: intact siding and ceiling materials are sealed with lead-safe painting at no extra charge. Removal, if ever needed, requires a licensed NJ asbestos contractor and is quoted separately.
  • After the sale: items raised by a future buyer’s inspector are not covered, except our own workmanship, which is covered by our warranty.
  • Staging, listing photos and utilities during work.

Payment & escrow

HOW YOUR MONEY IS PROTECTED

A proposed structure in which funds move only against an approved budget and verified progress.

  1. Client selects a package.
  2. Final scope, materials allowance, price and target completion date are placed in the written agreement.
  3. Project funds are held through a neutral escrow arrangement where agreed by the parties.
  4. Materials funds are released according to the approved budget.
  5. Receipts are maintained.
  6. Labor is paid according to verified project stages.
  7. Final payment occurs after the final walkthrough, punch list, applicable permit closeout and required resale inspection/closeout.
  8. Unused approved funds and contingency are reconciled and returned or credited according to the final agreement.

Due diligence

BEFORE WE START

Items to verify before signing and before work begins. These are project due-diligence items, not legal conclusions.

  • New A/C and furnace sizing is confirmed by the licensed NJ HVAC contractor before equipment is ordered.
  • Siding and old ceiling tiles may contain asbestos. This is accounted for in the plan and price: both are prepared and painted as part of the painting scope, with appropriate precautions.
  • Lead-safe / EPA RRP requirements apply because the home was built in 1958.
  • The sewer line has not yet been camera-scoped.
  • The shower valve/cartridge is currently failed and leaking.
  • NJ Home Improvement Contractor registration and the contracting structure must be confirmed before signing.
  • NJ-licensed trade contractors must handle applicable HVAC, electrical and plumbing work.
  • The final NJ contract should receive appropriate legal review.
  • Mount Ephraim resale CO checklist reviewed against the scope before work begins.
  • Permit and inspection requirements must be confirmed with the Borough or other appropriate authority.
